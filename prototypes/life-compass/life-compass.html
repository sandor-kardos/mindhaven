Mindhaven Life Compass

Personal Counselling · Mindhaven.uk · This worksheet stays on your device.

1. Values – What truly matters to you?

Write 3–5 keywords for each area.

2. Current situation – Where are you now?

On a scale from 1–10, how much does your life reflect these values right now?

3. Directions – If you lived by your values…

One sentence for each area: “If I lived more by my values here, my life would look like…”

4. Next steps – Small, realistic actions

Choose one small, doable step for each area.

5. Possible obstacles & supports

What might get in the way? And what could support you when that happens?

6. Anchor sentence

Write one short sentence that reminds you why this matters.

Your entries are stored only in your browser (localStorage). They are not sent to Mindhaven or any server.
